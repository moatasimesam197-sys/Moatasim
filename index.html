Services

Everything between the design and the stage: the console, the media server and the screen.

  • Lighting Programming

    Cue lists, effects and looks programmed on the console, built to run the same way every night.

    grandMA3 / grandMA2 / Avolites Titan

  • Media Server Programming

    Clips, layers and effects organised for live playback, triggered from the console or from timecode.

    Resolume Arena

  • LED Screen & Pixel Mapping

    Content mapped to your screens and fixtures so every pixel lands where the design intends.

    LED walls / Pixel mapping

  • Real-Time Visuals

    Generative visuals that respond to the music as it plays.

    TouchDesigner

  • 3D Content Design

    Custom 3D scenes and animations made for your stage and your LED screens.

    Blender

  • Timecode & Music Sync

    Lighting and video locked to the track, cue by cue, so the show repeats exactly.

    Timecode / Audio sync

  • Show Programming for Concerts & DJ Events

    Full lighting and visual programming for concerts, club nights and festivals.

    Lighting + visuals

Approach

Light, video and music should move as one

I take the design and turn it into programmed cues, clips and mapped content, so the show on stage matches the show on paper.
